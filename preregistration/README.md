## What the dates rest on

These texts were not committed to this repository, or deposited with a registry, at the time they were written.
H1–H5 were written in the authors' private review document and are exported here unchanged; H6 was written as a
local file. The dates above are the dates recorded in those texts and in the document's revision history. The git
history of this repository shows only that the earlier investigation (`legacy_preliminary/`, tag
`v0.1-preliminary`) predates all of them; it does not independently timestamp the hypothesis texts.
